Clip Uploader

Clip Uploader is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

What it does

  • Signs in with Google so it can upload videos to the YouTube channel the owner chooses.
  • Uploads one clip at a time, with a title, description and tags, every few hours.
  • Runs only on the owner's computer. There is no server, and no one else uses this app.

It is not a public service, and there is nothing to sign up for.
